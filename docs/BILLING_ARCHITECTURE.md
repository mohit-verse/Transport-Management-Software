# Billing Architecture

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the billing architecture for the Shri Sanwariya Road Lines (SRL) application.

The Billing Module manages:

- Company billing.
- Individual billing.
- Consolidated billing.
- Bill numbering.
- Bill versions.
- Bill status.
- Billing configuration.
- Bill cancellation.
- Bill/payment relationships.
- Bill document generation.
- Historical bill information.

The Billing Module must remain synchronized with Trips and the Payment Module.

---

# 2. Billing Scope

Billing applies to Company/Vendorship trips.

The system must support:

- Individual Bills.
- Consolidated Bills.
- Company-specific billing configuration.
- Company-specific numbering series.
- Company-specific billing templates.
- Bill versioning.
- Bill cancellation.
- Payment-linked bill settlement.

Market Party trips do not require the Company billing workflow unless explicitly introduced in a future requirement.

---

# 3. Billing Eligibility

A Trip can be selected for Company billing only when all required billing conditions are satisfied.

Minimum conditions:

1. Trip is Completed.
2. POD has been received.
3. Trip is currently Unbilled.
4. Trip is not Cancelled.
5. Required billing information is available.

Billing must not be allowed before POD receipt.

---

# 4. Bill Creation Workflow

The basic workflow is:

```text
Trip Completed
      ↓
POD Received
      ↓
Trip becomes Billing Eligible
      ↓
User selects Completed + Unbilled Trips
      ↓
Billing Configuration is loaded
      ↓
User selects Numbering Series
      ↓
Bill is generated
      ↓
Bill Version created
      ↓
Trip becomes Billed
      ↓
Bill can be Submitted
      ↓
Payment Module records payment
      ↓
Bill becomes Paid when fully accounted
```

---

# 5. Billing Mode

Each Company has a configured billing mode.

Allowed modes:

- Individual
- Consolidated

## Individual

One Bill contains one Trip.

## Consolidated

One Bill contains multiple selected Trips.

The selected Trips appear as individual line/items within the Bill.

---

# 6. Company Billing Configuration

Billing configuration belongs to the Company/Party.

Configuration includes:

- Billing Mode.
- Billing Template.
- Numbering Series.
- Required Billing Fields.
- TDS Treatment.

Only the Owner can modify billing configuration.

Staff can use the existing configuration but cannot modify it.

CA has view-only access.

---

# 7. Billing Configuration Changes

The Owner can change billing configuration at any time.

Future Bills use the new configuration.

Existing Bills remain unchanged.

Changing:

- Billing Mode.
- Billing Template.
- Numbering Series.
- Required Billing Fields.
- TDS Treatment.

must not modify historical Bills.

---

# 8. Bill Numbering

A Company can have multiple numbering series.

The user selects the numbering series when generating a Bill.

There is no default numbering series.

Each numbering series has:

- Configurable Prefix.
- Sequential Number.
- Financial Year.

---

# 9. Financial Year Number Reset

Bill numbering resets every Financial Year.

Example:

```text
FY 2026-27
SRL/26-27/001
SRL/26-27/002
SRL/26-27/003

FY 2027-28
SRL/27-28/001
SRL/27-28/002
SRL/27-28/003
```

The sequence starts again for each Financial Year.

Cancelled Bill numbers are never reused.

---

# 10. Bill Number Generation

Bill numbers must be generated by the system.

Users must not manually enter the final Bill Number.

The system must:

1. Identify the selected Company.
2. Identify the selected Financial Year.
3. Require the user to select an active numbering series.
4. Apply the configured prefix.
5. Generate the next sequential number.
6. Persist the generated number.
7. Never reuse the number.

Bill number generation must be transaction-safe so that concurrent users cannot receive the same Bill Number.

---

# 11. Bill Status

Only the following Bill statuses are allowed:

- Generated
- Submitted
- Paid
- Cancelled

The system must not introduce:

- Draft
- Partially Paid

as Bill statuses.

---

# 12. Generated

`Generated` means:

- The Bill has been created.
- A valid Bill Number exists.
- The Bill document has been generated.
- The Bill has not yet been marked Submitted.
- The Bill is not fully paid.

---

# 13. Submitted

`Submitted` means the Bill has been submitted/sent to the Company.

Submission information should preserve the relevant submission action/history.

A Submitted Bill can still have an outstanding amount.

---

# 14. Paid

`Paid` is system-derived.

A Bill becomes `Paid` only when the full Bill amount has been accounted for through active Payment allocations.

The user must not manually mark a Bill as Paid.

If a Payment is reversed, the Bill status must be recalculated.

---

# 15. Cancelled

`Cancelled` means the Bill has been cancelled by an authorized user.

Cancellation requires:

- Cancellation reason.
- Cancelled By.
- Cancellation Date/Time.

A Cancelled Bill cannot be restored.

---

# 16. Partial Payment

Bills do not have a `Partially Paid` status.

If a Bill has received only part of its amount:

- Its outstanding amount is reduced.
- Its status remains `Generated` or `Submitted`.
- It becomes `Paid` only after the complete Bill amount is accounted for through active Payment allocations.

---

# 17. Bill and Payment Relationship

The Payment Module is the authoritative source for actual money received.

A Bill only references Payment records.

The Bill must not create a separate payment ledger.

When a Payment is allocated to a Bill:

- Bill accounted amount increases.
- Bill outstanding decreases.
- Related Trip financial position updates.
- Bill status is recalculated.

---

# 18. Bill Payment Allocation

Payments may be associated with:

- A specific Bill.
- A specific Trip.
- Bulk/FIFO Party/Company receivables.

When a Bill-specific Payment is created:

```text
Payment
   ↓
Payment Allocation
   ↓
Bill
   ↓
Related Trip(s)
```

For a consolidated Bill, the Payment is allocated to the Bill, while the Bill maintains its relationship with its individual Trips.

---

# 19. Bill Amount

The Bill amount must be generated from the approved financial information of its included Trip(s).

The Bill must not manually invent a different financial amount from the underlying Trip unless an explicitly supported billing adjustment exists.

For a consolidated Bill:

`Bill Amount = Sum of Included Billable Trip Amounts`

The exact financial components included in the Bill depend on the approved billing configuration/template.

---

# 20. Individual Bill

An Individual Bill contains exactly one Trip.

Workflow:

```text
Eligible Trip
     ↓
Select Trip
     ↓
Generate Individual Bill
     ↓
Bill contains one Trip
```

The Trip becomes associated with the Bill.

The Trip cannot be included in another active Bill unless the original Bill is cancelled according to the cancellation workflow.

---

# 21. Consolidated Bill

A Consolidated Bill contains multiple selected Trips.

Each selected Trip appears as an individual bill line/item.

The system must preserve the relationship:

```text
Company
   ↓
Consolidated Bill
   ├── Trip 1
   ├── Trip 2
   ├── Trip 3
   └── ...
```

The user manually selects the Trips to include.

The system must not automatically include all outstanding Trips unless a future requirement explicitly enables such behavior.

---

# 22. Trip Billing State

A Trip must be distinguishable as:

- Unbilled.
- Billed.

A Trip becomes Billed after successful Bill generation.

A Trip returns to Unbilled if its active Bill is cancelled.

A Trip must not belong to multiple active Bills.

---

# 23. Bill Versioning

Bills support versioning.

Example:

```text
Bill SRL/26-27/001
    ├── v1
    ├── v2
    └── v3
```

The Bill Number remains:

`SRL/26-27/001`

The version changes.

---

# 24. Version Rules

When a Bill is corrected:

- Do not create a new Bill Number.
- Create a new Bill Version.
- Increment the version number.
- Preserve the previous version.
- Make the new version current.
- Make previous versions read-only.

Example:

```text
Bill: SRL/26-27/001

v1 → Previous
v2 → Current
```

---

# 25. Bill Version Data

Each Bill Version should preserve:

- Version Number.
- Bill data used at generation.
- Template reference.
- Generated document reference.
- Creation user.
- Creation date/time.
- Change reason where applicable.

The previous version must remain reconstructable/readable even after a newer version becomes current.

---

# 26. Bill Version and Payment

Payment allocations must reference the Bill rather than creating a separate financial obligation for every Bill Version.

A new Bill Version changes the presentation/content of the same Bill.

It does not create a new Bill Number.

Payment history must remain associated with the Bill.

---

# 27. Bill Cancellation

Only an authorized user may cancel a Bill.

Cancellation requires:

- Confirmation.
- Mandatory cancellation reason.
- User identity.
- Cancellation date/time.

After cancellation:

1. Bill status becomes Cancelled.
2. Associated Trips return to Unbilled.
3. The Bill cannot be restored.
4. A new Bill can be generated from the eligible Trips.

---

# 28. Cancelled Bill Storage

To reduce unnecessary storage:

The full active Bill document/details are removed from active storage after cancellation.

Permanent lightweight cancellation metadata remains.

The retained metadata includes:

- Bill Number.
- Cancellation status.
- Cancellation reason.
- Cancelled By.
- Cancellation Date/Time.
- Related Company.
- Relevant historical identifiers.

The cancelled Bill is not retained as a full active document/history object.

---

# 29. Cancelled Bill Numbers

Cancelled Bill Numbers are never reused.

Example:

```text
SRL/26-27/001 → Cancelled

Next Bill:
SRL/26-27/002
```

The system must never generate:

`SRL/26-27/001`

again.

---

# 30. Billing Template

Every Company has one configured billing template at a time.

The template can be changed later by the Owner.

Existing Bills are not changed when the template changes.

Future Bills use the current configured template.

---

# 31. Billing Template Capabilities

The structured Billing Template must support:

- Text.
- Tables.
- Borders.
- Lines.
- Dynamic Fields.
- Images.
- Logo.
- Signature.
- Stamp.
- Page Headers.
- Page Footers.
- Spacing.
- Alignment.
- Repeatable Trip Rows.
- Calculated Fields.
- Page Breaks.

The template must support multi-page Bills.

---

# 32. Dynamic Fields

The Bill Designer must provide dynamic fields from the approved structured data model.

Fields should be organized by source.

Potential sources include:

- Party/Company.
- Trip.
- Vehicle.
- Vehicle Owner where applicable.
- Destination.
- Billing data.
- Approved financial data.
- Other approved master data.

The system must not restrict the Bill Designer to a tiny fixed set of manually hard-coded tags.

---

# 33. Consolidated Template Rows

Consolidated billing requires repeatable Trip rows/sections.

The template must support a repeatable structure similar to:

```text
Trip 1
Trip 2
Trip 3
...
Trip N
```

The system must be able to expand the repeatable section across pages when required.

The template must not depend on a fixed number of Trip rows.

---

# 34. Existing Bill Reconstruction

The Bill Designer must support importing an existing reference bill.

Supported input formats:

- PDF.
- JPG.
- PNG.

The workflow is:

```text
Upload Existing Bill
        ↓
Automatic Reconstruction
        ↓
Editable Structured Elements
        ↓
User Review
        ↓
User Correction
        ↓
Save Structured Template
        ↓
Generate Bills from Template
```

The system must automatically reconstruct the editable structure.

Manual reconstruction is not a separate starting workflow.

---

# 35. Structured Template Requirement

An uploaded blank/reference Bill must not simply be stored as a fixed background image for final Bill generation.

The Bill must be generated from structured editable elements.

This is necessary because:

- Consolidated rows can vary in count.
- Tables can span multiple pages.
- Dynamic fields can change length.
- Content can cross page boundaries.

The saved template must therefore represent the Bill as structured content.

---

# 36. Bill Rendering

Bill rendering must use:

```text
Structured Template
        +
Business Data
        +
Selected Trips
        +
Billing Configuration
        ↓
Generated Bill
```

The renderer must support:

- Dynamic values.
- Variable-length content.
- Repeatable rows.
- Multi-page layouts.
- Page breaks.
- Headers and footers.
- Calculated values.

---

# 37. Generated Bill PDF

Generated Bill PDFs are stored by default.

Owner can delete stored generated PDFs when required.

Deleting a generated PDF must not delete:

- Bill record.
- Bill Number.
- Bill Version.
- Structured Bill data.
- Template.
- Payment relationship.

The PDF can be regenerated from the structured Bill data and template.

Staff can view/download generated PDFs but cannot delete them.

---

# 38. Bill Designer Assets

The Bill Designer must support:

- Company Logo.
- Stamp.
- Signature.
- Other approved billing images.

Assets must be stored independently from the Bill layout where appropriate so that templates can reference reusable assets.

---

# 39. Bill Regeneration

A Bill PDF may need to be regenerated when:

- PDF was deleted.
- Rendering needs to be repeated.
- A new Bill Version is created.

Regeneration must use the appropriate:

- Bill Version.
- Structured Template.
- Business Data.

Regeneration must not silently modify historical Bill Versions.

---

# 40. Billing Configuration Ownership

Only Owner can modify:

1. Billing Mode.
2. Billing Template.
3. Numbering Series.
4. Required Billing Fields.
5. TDS Treatment.

Staff can:

- View configuration.
- Use configuration.
- Generate Bills using configuration.

Staff cannot change configuration.

CA can view relevant billing information but cannot modify configuration.

---

# 41. Required Billing Fields

Each Company can have required billing fields configured by the Owner.

When generating a Bill, the system must validate that required information is available.

If required data is missing:

- Bill generation must be blocked.
- The missing field must be clearly identified.
- The user must be directed to the relevant source record.

The system must not silently generate an incomplete Bill when a configured required field is missing.

---

# 42. TDS Billing Treatment

Company billing configuration contains TDS Treatment.

The exact treatment must follow the configured Company requirement.

The system must not invent TDS rules for a Company where they have not been configured.

Actual TDS amount remains associated with the appropriate Trip/financial record.

---

# 43. Billing and POD

Billing before POD receipt is not allowed.

The system must validate POD status before allowing a Trip to be selected for Company billing.

POD receipt occurs when the POD file is uploaded.

The POD upload timestamp becomes the POD Received Date/Time.

---

# 44. Billing and Trip Changes

A Trip may require changes after billing.

If a change affects the Bill:

1. The existing Bill remains historically preserved.
2. A new Bill Version is created.
3. The updated Trip information is reflected in the new version.
4. The previous version becomes read-only.
5. The Bill Number remains unchanged.

A new Bill Number is not required for a correction to an existing Bill.

---

# 45. Billing and Trip Cancellation

A Trip that is part of an active Bill cannot be treated as independently Unbilled without resolving the Bill relationship.

If the Bill is cancelled:

- The Bill becomes Cancelled.
- Associated Trips return to Unbilled.
- Eligible Trips can be billed again.

Trip cancellation and Bill cancellation must not silently break financial relationships.

---

# 46. Bill List

The Bill List must support:

- Search.
- Combined filters.
- Desktop Table/Card toggle.
- Mobile compact cards.

Relevant filters include:

- Company.
- Bill Number.
- Financial Year.
- Bill Status.
- Billing Mode.
- Bill Date.
- Payment status/position where applicable.

The exact visible list fields should remain limited to approved UI requirements.

---

# 47. Bill Details

Bill Details should provide:

## Basic Information

- Bill Number.
- Bill Version.
- Current/Previous Version.
- Bill Date.
- Company.
- Billing Mode.
- Status.

## Trip Information

- Included Trips.
- Trip Numbers.
- Trip amounts.
- Trip-related billing information.

## Payment Information

- Related Payments.
- Payment allocations.
- Accounted amount.
- Outstanding amount.

## Document

- Current generated Bill PDF.
- Previous version documents where retained.
- Regeneration action where permitted.

## Audit

- Bill creation history.
- Version history.
- Submission history where available.
- Cancellation history.
- Relevant changes.

---

# 48. Bill Submission

Submission changes the Bill status from:

`Generated → Submitted`

The system must preserve the submission action in history.

Submission does not mean payment has been received.

The Bill remains Submitted until:

- It becomes Paid, or
- It is Cancelled.

---

# 49. Bill Payment Completion

The Bill becomes Paid only when:

`Total Active Allocated Payments >= Bill Amount`

The system must derive the status automatically.

If an active payment is reversed:

```text
Paid
  ↓
Payment Reversed
  ↓
Outstanding Recalculated
  ↓
Bill Status Recalculated
```

The Bill may return to `Generated` or `Submitted` depending on its previous submission state.

---

# 50. Billing Audit

Billing audit history must record important actions, including:

- Bill generation.
- Bill version creation.
- Bill editing.
- Bill regeneration.
- Bill submission.
- Bill cancellation.
- Billing configuration changes.
- Numbering series changes.
- Template changes.

Audit information should include:

- User.
- Role.
- Date/Time.
- Action.
- Previous value where applicable.
- New value where applicable.
- Reason where applicable.

---

# 51. Authorization Summary

## Owner

Full Billing access.

Can:

- Generate Bills.
- Edit Bills.
- Regenerate Bills.
- Create Bill Versions.
- Cancel Bills.
- Manage billing configuration.
- Manage templates.
- Manage numbering series.
- Delete generated Bill PDFs.

## Staff

Can:

- Generate Bills.
- Edit Bills.
- Regenerate Bills.
- Create Bill Versions.
- View Bills.
- Submit Bills where permitted.

Cannot:

- Modify billing configuration.
- Manage numbering series.
- Manage company billing templates.
- Delete generated Bill PDFs.

## CA

View-only billing access.

CA cannot:

- Generate Bills.
- Edit Bills.
- Regenerate Bills.
- Cancel Bills.
- Modify templates.
- Modify numbering.

---

# 52. Non-Negotiable Billing Rules

1. Company billing requires Completed + POD Received + Unbilled Trip.
2. Billing before POD is prohibited.
3. One Trip can belong to only one active Bill.
4. Individual Bill contains one Trip.
5. Consolidated Bill can contain multiple Trips.
6. Consolidated Trips remain individually identifiable inside the Bill.
7. Bill statuses are only Generated, Submitted, Paid, and Cancelled.
8. There is no Draft Bill status.
9. There is no Partially Paid Bill status.
10. Paid status is system-derived from active Payment allocations.
11. Payment records are controlled by the Payment Module.
12. Bill versions retain the same Bill Number.
13. Previous Bill versions are read-only.
14. Correcting a Bill creates a new version.
15. Cancelling a Bill never reuses its Bill Number.
16. Cancelled Bills cannot be restored.
17. Cancelled Bills release their Trips back to Unbilled.
18. Company billing configuration is Owner-only.
19. Company can have multiple numbering series.
20. User selects the numbering series when generating a Bill.
21. Numbering resets every Financial Year.
22. Existing Bills are not changed when billing configuration changes.
23. Generated PDFs are stored by default.
24. Deleting a PDF does not delete the structured Bill.
25. Bills must be rendered from structured templates.
26. A fixed background image cannot be the sole Bill-generation mechanism.
27. Consolidated templates must support repeatable Trip rows.
28. Bill/payment synchronization must use Payment Module records.
29. Billing actions must be auditable.
